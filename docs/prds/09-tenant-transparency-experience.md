# PRD-09: Tenant Transparency Experience

## Goal
Give tenants clear visibility into inspections, evidence, and outcomes tied to their room.

## Source alignment
- Master PRD sections: 6.4, 11.7.
- Functional requirements: FR-29 through FR-32.

## Scope
- Tenant home, inbox, inspections list, inspection detail, room info, settings.
- Read-only evidence and AI summary access.
- Timeline and status explanations in plain language.

## UX requirements
- Transparent without exposing privileged admin fields.
- Simple, mobile-friendly, low-cognitive-load layout.
- Clear empty states when inspections are not yet available.

## Acceptance criteria
- Tenant can see upcoming and completed inspections for their room.
- Tenant can view finalized report details and attached media.
- Tenant cannot modify inspection evidence or admin-only metadata.
- Navigation and terminology are understandable for first-time users.
